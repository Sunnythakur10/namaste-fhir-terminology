## Option 1: Technical Deep Dive (Recommended for Tech Audience)

🏥 **Bridging 5,000 Years of Ayurveda with Modern Healthcare Standards** 🏥

I'm excited to share my latest project: **NAMASTE-FHIR** — a microservice that brings traditional Ayurvedic medicine into the digital age while maintaining WHO ICD-11 compliance.

**🎯 The Challenge:**
How do you digitize ancient Ayurvedic diagnostic terms like "Madhumeha" (traditional term for Diabetes) while ensuring compatibility with modern Electronic Health Records and international health standards?

**💡 The Solution:**
I developed a FastAPI-based FHIR R4 microservice that provides:

✅ **Intelligent Terminology Mapping** - Fuzzy string matching handles Sanskrit transliteration variations, automatically mapping "Diabetes" → "Madhumeha/Kshaudrameha" with 100% confidence scoring

✅ **Dual ICD-11 Classification** - Simultaneous mapping to both ICD-11 Traditional Medicine Module 2 (TM2: SJ00) and Biomedicine codes (5A11) for the same condition

✅ **Real-time Analytics** - Population health surveillance across 9 Indian states, tracking 50+ patients with 6 major disease categories

✅ **FHIR-First Architecture** - Native CodeSystem, ConceptMap, ValueSet, and Observation resources for seamless EHR integration

**🔧 Technical Implementation:**
- **Backend:** FastAPI with async/await for sub-second response times
- **Security:** OAuth 2.0 with ABHA (Ayushman Bharat Health Account) token authentication
- **Search:** FuzzyWuzzy + Levenshtein distance for intelligent multi-field queries
- **Standards:** Full FHIR R4 compliance with WHO ICD-11 integration
- **Analytics:** Pandas-powered real-time morbidity statistics

**📊 Key Results:**
• 100% ICD-11 mapping coverage for Ayurvedic terminology
• Sub-second API response times for 1000+ term searches
• Production-ready system supporting Ministry of Ayush digital health initiatives
• Enables evidence-based traditional medicine practice

**🌟 Why This Matters:**
This project demonstrates that traditional knowledge systems can coexist with modern healthcare technology. By creating a standardized digital bridge, we enable:
- Ayurvedic practitioners to use familiar terminology while meeting global standards
- Researchers to conduct international studies on traditional medicine effectiveness
- Government health departments to track population health trends across traditional practices

**Tech Stack:** Python | FastAPI | FHIR R4 | ICD-11 WHO | OAuth 2.0 | Pandas | FuzzyWuzzy | RESTful APIs

Proud to contribute to India's Digital Health Mission while preserving our traditional medical heritage! 🇮🇳
